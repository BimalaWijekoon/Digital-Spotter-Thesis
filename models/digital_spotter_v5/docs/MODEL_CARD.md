# Model card — Digital Spotter V5 SCOPE_FAITHFUL

**Purpose.** Binary good/bad form classification for squat and deadlift repetitions from three phase-aggregated vision and lower-back IMU rows. The positive class is historical bad form (`Class_Binary=1`). This is research software, not a validated injury-prevention or medical device.

**Population and evaluation.** Source evidence contains 25 participants and 2,018 repetitions. Research generalisation evidence is five participant-held-out outer folds, each repeated with seeds 42–44 for the Stage 7D Scope-Faithful candidate. The selected candidate's pooled OOF F1/recall/PR-AUC mean ± SD are 0.921673 ± 0.001933 / 0.913522 ± 0.002968 / 0.918418 ± 0.001698. The per-seed metrics and original decision report are [final_oof_metrics.csv](../results/final_oof_metrics.csv) and [Stage 7D](V5_STAGE7D_SCOPE_PRESERVING_CONFIRMATION_REPORT.md). Subject- and exercise-level results remain visible in `results/`.

**Final architecture/input.** Vision+IMU, temporal `(1,3,29)` and context `(1,2)`, BiLSTM 64→32, two-head self-attention (`key_dim=16`), residual/LayerNorm/pooling, context Dense 16, fusion Dense 64→32, sigmoid. Total parameters: **105,681**. Context is `Exercise_ID`, `Load_kg`. The three hip/knee/ankle asymmetry features are included. No synthetic delta timestep, delta branch, anthropometrics, explicit missingness indicators, or unresolved velocity/power/jerk/deceleration proxy fields. The [feature contract](FEATURE_CONTRACT.md) is authoritative for order and preprocessing.

**Final deployment decisions.** Exactly one all-data Keras model was trained on all 2,018 historical-target repetitions with plain BCE, seed 42, and nine fixed epochs. One Platt calibrator was fitted to selected-loss outer-OOF raw probabilities/labels. The deployment threshold is **0.20549274582373653**, chosen to maximize F1 on those globally calibrated OOF labels. These fitted decisions are documented in [Stage 8](V5_STAGE8_FINAL_DEPLOYMENT_MODEL_REPORT.md) and the files under `saved_models/`.

**No independent final-model estimate.** The final all-data deployment model is **not independently evaluated**, because all available repetitions are used for deployment training. Participant-held-out OOF evidence is the research generalisation estimate. Stage 8 loss-policy selection itself consulted outer OOF labels; fit-data Brier/F1 from the global calibrator/threshold is descriptive and cannot be reported as a new unbiased result. The OOF-fitted calibrator's transfer to the all-data model probability scale is not independently validated.

**Target-label limitation.** 374/2,018 repetitions have phase-level `Class_Binary` inconsistencies. `V5_REPRO_TARGET` uses the historical phase-3 label for continuity, not a verified original repetition-level annotation. No label was relabelled or reconstructed from error-type fields.

**Interpretability limits.** Stage 7D attention is close to uniform over three phases; weights are descriptive learned weighting, not causal reasoning. Stage 7D Integrated Gradients gave the asymmetry fields modest attribution. Retention reflects practical equivalence and the intended biomechanical representation, **not** proof that asymmetry is essential or attention improves accuracy. See [FIGURE_SOURCES.md](FIGURE_SOURCES.md).

**Conversion/deployment.** Final FP32 and dynamic-range, batch-one, built-ins-only TFLite files passed local full-TensorFlow-interpreter parity against Keras. INT8 conversion did not produce an artifact. The final package has no portable Pi preprocessing/runtime, no Pi `tflite-runtime` load result, and no measured end-to-end synchronization, inference latency, CPU, memory, or thermal result. See [TFLITE_STATUS.md](../saved_models/tflite/TFLITE_STATUS.md).

**Unsupported claims.** Do not claim independently validated performance of the final all-data model, unseen-exercise generalisation, true bar-motion sensing, causal explanation from attention/attribution, successful Raspberry Pi deployment, or hardware performance benchmarks.
